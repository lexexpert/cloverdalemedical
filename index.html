FAQs

Got Questions? Find Answers About Our Healthcare Services

What are the operating hours of Cloverdale Medical Centre?

We are open Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM; Wednesday and Friday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM; Saturday from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM; and Sunday from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

Do I need an appointment to visit the clinic?

Appointments are recommended, but walk-ins are welcome for urgent care.

What kind of insurance do you accept?

Cloverdale Medical Centre accepts most major health insurance plans. Please contact our front desk to confirm whether your insurance is accepted or discuss payment options for uncovered services.

Can I access my medical records online?

No, online access to medical records is not available. Please visit the office to request your medical records in person.

Is there parking available at the clinic?

Yes, we have ample free parking available for patients directly in front of the medical centre.

Do you offer telehealth services?

No, Cloverdale Medical Centre does not provide telehealth services. We offer in-person care only.
